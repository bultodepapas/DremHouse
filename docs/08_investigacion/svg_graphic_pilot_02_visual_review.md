## 1. Review outcome

GP02 passes as the second graphic-language prototype. It confirms that the candidate
`1684 × 1191` frame can support a sparse elevation without reproducing the source
sheet's large unused upper field or forcing explanatory text into the façade.

The elevation now carries only stable opening IDs, modules, essential dimensions and
level references. Full adopted-opening evidence, the excluded dining-window study and
open professional gates occupy three separate lower panels. The optional dining study
does not enter the façade geometry and cannot be mistaken for an adopted opening.

GP02 remains presentation evidence only. It does not pass SVG-G0 or SVG-G1, does not
authorise the remaining pilot sheets and must not be promoted to `planos/actual/`.

## 2. Changes implemented

- Copied the 60 source content nodes from the current R10 elevation into one documented
  uniform sheet transform; no non-text geometry coordinate was edited.
- Increased the useful elevation scale and moved evidence below the view rather than
  filling the sheet with an oversized title or tutorial prose.
- Added stable `data-model-id` values for the Side B envelope, four opening families,
  structural-grid references, external level and longitudinal dimensions.
- Replaced long text inside small openings with direct IDs: `GLZ-RC`, `GLZ-WS-B`,
  `PB-WS-B`, `W-H2` and `W-G`.
- Retained adopted dimensions, sills, head levels and module counts in a readable lower
  evidence panel under D-083.
- Shortened the upper-floor extent note to `P2 · 15.00 m` so it fits between the two
  bedroom openings without crossing their graphics.
- Placed `GLZ-DINING-STUDY-B` in a purple dashed `NOT ADOPTED` panel and stated that the
  dining bay remains solid in the elevation.
- Placed unresolved site, solar/privacy, safe-glazing/fall, structural/wind,
  building-physics/drainage and product/cost items in an amber dashed `OPEN · DO NOT
  FREEZE` panel.
- Reused a new shared accessible document root, semantic theme, header, authority footer
  and text helpers from `dreamhouse/svg/sheet.py`.
- Added `dreamhouse/svg/audit.py` so colour, grayscale, reduced-size, before/after and
  text-size evidence can be regenerated for later pilots through one command.

## 4. Visual review record

### 4.1 Full colour at 1,400 px

Passed for pilot use:

- the elevation, high-eave datum and 36.00 m length read before the evidence panels;
- opening IDs and module logic remain attached to the correct glazing;
- the lower panels distinguish adopted, excluded and open information immediately;
- the solid domestic façade is not overlaid with the excluded dining opening; and
- document identity, pilot status and no-construction authority are immediately visible.

### 4.2 Grayscale at 1,400 px

Passed for pilot use. The dark glazing, dashed P2 reference, panel borders, explicit
`NOT ADOPTED` and `OPEN · DO NOT FREEZE` wording, and dark authority footer preserve the
information hierarchy without relying on hue.

### 4.3 Reduced overview at 800 px

Passed as a navigation/overview image. Sheet title, elevation silhouette, opening
distribution, four direct IDs, three evidence states and authority footer remain
recognisable. Evidence paragraphs remain zoom content.

### 4.4 Defects found and corrected during the loop

1. `GLZ-WS-B` initially retained white source text after its label moved onto the light
   façade. It now uses dark ink and passes visual contrast review.
2. The long `REAR UPPER FLOOR · 15.00 m` label crossed both bedroom-opening graphics. It
   became the shorter `P2 · 15.00 m` direct datum, while the meaning remains unchanged.
3. The structural reference at X=31.50 crossed the original guest-window sentence. The
   direct `W-G · 3 × 1.20` label was shortened and moved within the same opening.

## 6. Known limitations and next batch

- GP02 still adapts a flat predecessor SVG. It is not yet a family renderer backed by
  typed model and annotation objects.
- Collision resolution remains curated. The fail-closed label registry planned under
  WP1 is not yet implemented.
- The source nodes retain some presentation literals. Full palette and lineweight
  tokenisation should follow after the graphic direction is accepted.
- The visual audit measures font sizes and produces review images, but contrast,
  bounding-box and collision reports remain future increments.
- Cross-browser font metrics remain an acceptance gate.
- The next pilot should address `DH-ARQ-SEC-002`, the legacy transverse section without a
  `viewBox`, using the same shared frame and repeatable visual-audit command.
