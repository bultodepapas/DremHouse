## Hard rules modified by a later decision

| Hard rule | Original wording                                                                                                    | Current modification                                                                                                                           | Decision              |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| 9         | “The usable bedrooms of the two children will have **exactly the same area**, with qualitative equivalence sought.” | Equal within **±1.00 m² of usable area**. Qualitative equivalence is measured: both aspect ratios ≤1.35:1 and difference between ratios ≤0.30. | **D-042**, 2026-08-11 |

The Constitution requires every change to a hard rule to be recorded together with its
effect. D-042 allowed Phase 2 of P2 to be reorganized so the guest bedroom could meet the
program at 17.0 m² gross versus the required 17–18 m², its bathroom could increase from
1.70 m to 2.00 m clear depth, and the construction vestibule could exceed the minimum
circulation width. Without the tolerance, those three improvements were mutually
incompatible.
