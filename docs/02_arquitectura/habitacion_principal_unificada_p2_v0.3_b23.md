## Decision

D-067 identifies the former sleep-zone and lounge/entry polygons as one continuous
**Primary bedroom** of **35.24 m² gross**. The two polygons remain only as a parametric
tessellation of its L-shaped footprint; the plan shows one room name and one area value.

The 1.45 m fitted privacy screen introduced under D-065 is removed physically from the
layout, including its symbol and label. No wall, door, circulation route, window or room
area changes.

## Coordination effect

The room now reads more simply and generously. Entry remains from the family distributor,
the dressing and bathroom retain their own doors, and the bed remains in the quieter
south/east portion. Without the screen, the direct sightline from the suite entrance must
be assessed through bed orientation, curtains, lighting and loose furniture at the 1:25
interior-design stage rather than by reinstating an assumed fixed barrier.

Removing one small fitted element may reduce joinery scope, but no saving is recognized
until the final furniture and electrical layouts are measured and quoted. D-067 does not
change the construction target or authorize procurement or construction.
