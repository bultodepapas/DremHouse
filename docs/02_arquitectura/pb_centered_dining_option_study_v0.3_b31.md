# PB centred 12-seat dining option study — b31

**Status:** owner-directed evidence-based refinement; option only; not adopted and not for construction  
**Version:** 0.3-option-31-PB  
**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** 12-seat dining-table size, chair topology, clearance envelope and true centring in the Side B domestic territory  
**Source:** owner instruction of 2026-08-21; PB b30 option; active PB b29 and D-071

## Architectural conclusion

Use a **3.20 × 1.10 × 0.75 m rectangular table** for twelve people. Arrange five chairs
along each long side and one chair at each head. Centre the complete symmetric group at
**X=26.25 m, Y=14.41 m**, the geometric centre of the 10.50 × 6.82 m Side B dining
territory.

This is deliberately smaller than the inherited 3.60 × 1.30 m envelope. The previous
table was plausible as an oversized communal or banquet table, but it was larger than
necessary for twelve and remained visibly displaced toward the central axis.

## Internet research

The sources do not prescribe one universal dimension because seating capacity also
depends on table legs, pedestal position, chair width and armrests. They nevertheless
establish a useful commercial range:

- West Elm's table-sizing guide assigns **120 in (3.05 m) to 12 seats**, recommends
  approximately **24 in (0.61 m) per person**, and asks for **3 ft (0.91 m)** around a
  table. Its more detailed dining-room guidance expands the chair zone to **36–44 in
  (0.91–1.12 m)**. [West Elm sizing guide](https://www.westelm.com/pages/ideas-and-advice/what-size-dining-table/)
- West Elm's 12-seat Blakley product uses a **120 in (3.05 m)** length with either
  **39 in (0.99 m)** standard depth or **45 in (1.14 m)** extra depth.
  [West Elm Blakley table](https://aktest2-www.westelm.com/products/mp-blakley-dining-table-60-120-mp052/)
- Pottery Barn lists a **120 × 52 in (3.05 × 1.32 m)** Keaton table for **10–12 people**,
  confirming that deeper tables exist but are not required merely to reach twelve seats.
  [Pottery Barn Keaton table](https://www.potterybarn.com/products/keaton-communal-wood-dining-table/pip-print.html)
- IKEA similarly recommends approximately **24 in per diner** and at least **32 in
  (0.81 m)** behind the table, noting that more is preferable.
  [IKEA dining-table guide](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/rooms/dining/how-to/3-tips-for-buying-a-dining-table-pubf11224c6/)

The selected 3.20 m length provides **0.64 m per long-side chair**, slightly more than the
0.61 m benchmark, without reaching the 3.60–3.66 m scale used by some 12-seat communal
products. The 1.10 m depth sits between standard and extra-deep commercial examples: it
supports shared dishes while preserving conversation and reach across the table.

Relative to b30, the table moves **0.25 m toward the Great Wall** and **0.76 m toward Side
B**, while reducing by 0.40 m in length and 0.20 m in depth. Because the chair arrangement
and clearance envelope are symmetric, centring the tabletop also centres the complete
dining group.

## Architectural and technical notes

- Use either a pedestal/trestle arrangement or legs close to the corners so five side
  chairs fit without striking supports. Nominal tabletop capacity alone is insufficient.
- Select real chair widths and armrest conditions with the table. The 0.64 m allocation
  is comfortable for ordinary dining chairs but must be rechecked for broad armchairs.
- Centre a linear pendant or a balanced pendant group on the final table, not on the
  boundaries of the much larger dining territory. Keep the circuit and structural support
  adjustable until the furniture position is adopted.
- A rug is optional. If used, it should remain within the 5.40 × 3.30 m clear envelope and
  extend far enough that rear chair legs do not leave it when occupied.
- The surrounding residual area is intentionally open. Do not fill it with decorative
  cabinets merely because the plan shows generous margins.

## Cost and governance

The table and chairs remain loose owner furniture; b31 does not alter the physical-works
cost baseline. A future linear pendant, floor outlet or fixed sideboard would create
building scope and must be measured separately before adoption.

PB b31 supersedes b30 only for the dining-table envelope, seating arrangement and
centring. PB b29 remains the active published ground-floor plan, and D-071 still governs
its beside-kitchen dining position. This study does not create D-074 or modify D-024,
CF-006, the current drawing catalog or the cost target.
