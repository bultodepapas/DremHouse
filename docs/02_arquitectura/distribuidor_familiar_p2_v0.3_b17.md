## Spatial decision

P2 b17/R14 removes the double-corridor condition left in R13. The former 2.90 × 3.60 m
protected arrival is absorbed into a furnished **10.50 × 3.60 m family distributor
(37.80 m² gross)** reached directly from the stair. Seating and the fitted library occupy
the room side; a clear edge distributes occupants to the primary suite, mini deck and the
Phase 2 extension.

The former 15.00 × 1.45 m Phase 2 lobby is divided into:

- a **10.50 × 1.45 m open family study/distribution edge (15.225 m² gross)**, continuous
  with the main family distributor after Phase 2 completion; and
- one **4.50 × 1.45 m short wellness/egress spur (6.525 m² gross)**.

Explicit common corridor area therefore reduces from 32.19 m² gross in R13—the former
arrival plus full Phase 2 lobby—to 6.525 m² gross in R14. The recovered area remains useful
shared living/study space rather than residual circulation.

## Phasing

Y=11.00 m remains the single isolatable construction boundary. During Phase 1, a temporary
sealed partition closes that line. When Phase 2 is completed, its portion across the family
centre is removed to join the main room and study edge. The 4.50 m short spur remains
enclosed to serve wellness and the reserved exterior route.

## Fire and acoustic hold point

The direct stair arrival expresses the owner's preferred domestic experience; it does not
establish an approved fire strategy. A responsible professional must determine the required
stair enclosure, smoke control, ratings, doors and travel strategy. If separation is
required, use the smallest compliant rated or glazed vestibule integrated with the stair—
not a reinstated parallel corridor.

Child Suite 1 still enters through the mini deck. Its correction remains a separate open
architectural action and is not silently included in D-061.

## Cost and authority

study counter and potentially specialist fire-rated glazing/doors. These effects remain
unmeasured and unquoted. D-061 changes neither the cost target nor contingency. The sheets
are coordination evidence only and are not suitable for procurement or construction.
