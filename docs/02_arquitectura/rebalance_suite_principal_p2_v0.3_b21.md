# Primary-suite rebalance — draft 21

**Status:** active schematic coordination hypothesis under D-065; privacy, joinery and
façade detail remain open; not for construction<br>
**Version:** 0.3-draft-21-P2 / R18<br>
**Date:** 2026-08-18<br>
**Source:** explicit owner approval following analysis of the R17 primary dressing/filter.

## Spatial decision

D-065 relocates the primary dressing from the north side of the bedroom to the west side,
against the Child 1 wardrobe/bathroom service band. Its **3.20 m width** follows the
existing primary-bathroom service-wall dimension and its **4.20 m depth** follows the
bedroom band.

The unchanged 66.28 m² gross primary private-room allocation is redistributed as follows:

| Component | R17 gross | R18 gross | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Primary bedroom | 31.08 m² | **35.24 m²** | +4.16 m² |
| Dressing/filter | 17.60 m² | **13.44 m²** | −4.16 m² |
| L-shaped bathroom | 17.60 m² | **17.60 m²** | 0.00 m² |
| **Total** | **66.28 m²** | **66.28 m²** | **0.00 m²** |

The former dressing becomes the bedroom lounge/entry zone. A **4.20 m opening** removes
the former horizontal wall over the full sleep-zone width. The dressing retains two
0.60 m wardrobe runs with approximately 1.75 m schematic space between them before
detailed linings and joinery tolerances.

## Access and privacy

The suite is now entered through the bedroom lounge rather than through clothing storage,
resolving the circulation concern identified in the R12 architectural review. A short
1.45 m fitted privacy screen interrupts the direct sightline from the family distributor
toward the bed without recreating a full filter room.

The bathroom remains entered from the lounge/entry zone. The dressing receives its own
0.90 m door. Detailed door swings, wardrobes, mirrors, seating, lighting and simultaneous
use require a 1:25 interior layout.

## Window coordination

The south bedroom window starts at X=32.05 instead of X=30.00, reducing that opening from
5.50 m to **3.45 m** so glazing does not cross the dressing. The 2.50 m east bedroom window
remains unchanged. Daylight, façade rhythm, structure, guarding, curtains and thermal
performance remain open coordination items.

## Cost and authority

The change removes and rebuilds internal partitions, changes doors and fitted wardrobes,
adds a privacy screen and shortens/repositions one exterior window. No net saving or
increase is recognized until wall, joinery, glazing, façade and electrical quantities are
remeasured and quoted. The drawing is not suitable for procurement or construction.
