## Graphic decision

P2 b20/R17 removes the purple F1/F2 phase-boundary line, its label and its legend key
from the main upper-floor architectural plan because they compete visually with the
spatial reading of the family balcony and rooms.

This is a presentation change only. The Y=11.00 m construction-phase boundary remains:

- active in the parametric model and validation report;
- visible in `DH-ARQ-DIA-001`, the dedicated access/egress and phasing diagram; and
- mandatory for Phase 1 temporary enclosure, guarding, fire, dust, noise and services
  coordination.

R17 changes no geometry, areas, programme, materials, construction scope, cost target or
professional-design gate. R16/D-063 remains the spatial basis.
