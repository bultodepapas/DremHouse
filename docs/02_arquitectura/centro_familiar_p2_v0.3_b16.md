# Coherent P2 family centre — draft 16

**Status:** superseded by D-061/P2 b17-R14; retained as the direct predecessor<br>
**Version:** 0.3-draft-16-P2 / R13<br>
**Date:** 2026-08-18<br>
**Source:** owner instruction to implement the first recommendation in the R12 upper-floor
architectural review; P2 b15/R12; D-042, D-048 and D-050–D-060.

## Decision implemented

P2 b16/R13 replaces the fragmented family lounge, 1.10 m gallery/library and private hall
with one recognizable **7.60 × 3.60 m family room (27.36 m² gross)**. Circulation is
absorbed along the east and north edges of the room instead of being assigned separate
floor-area compartments. A **4.20 m fitted library wall** replaces the dedicated gallery
strip.

The acoustic mini deck contracts from 2.80 × 3.50 m to **2.80 × 2.40 m**. It remains a
distinct room and the only scheduled visual connection through P2-W04 toward the double-
height hall. Its reduced depth releases the full 3.60 m family-room depth without changing
the 18.00 × 15.00 m P2 envelope.

## Controls retained

- exactly four ensuite bedrooms on P2;
- the unchanged Child 1 and Child 2 bedroom geometry and D-042 equivalence checks;
- the primary-suite geometry adopted under D-050;
- the single Phase 1 / Phase 2 boundary at Y=11.00 m;
- the protected-stair and four D-048 column reservations;
- P2-W01, P2-W04 and P2-W05 under D-057–D-059; and
- the open professional fire, structure, acoustics, envelope and site-orientation gates.

## Deliberate limit of this iteration

Child Suite 1 still enters through the mini deck. This issue implements only the first
architectural-review recommendation. Moving that bedroom entrance is not resolved silently
and remains a separate high-priority design action.

## Cost and authority

The new layout removes residual circulation subdivisions and may reduce partition, door and
acoustic-glazing quantities, while adding a deliberate fitted-library element. These effects
remain unmeasured and unquoted. D-060 changes neither the cost target nor the contingency.
The issued sheets are coordination evidence only and are not suitable for procurement or
construction.
