# Unified primary bathroom — draft 22

**Status:** retained D-066 bathroom basis; superseded by D-067/P2 b23-R20 only as the
current published issue; waterproofing, drainage, ventilation, privacy and detailed MEP
remain open; not for construction<br>
**Version:** 0.3-draft-22-P2 / R19<br>
**Date:** 2026-08-18<br>
**Source:** explicit owner instruction to unify the primary bathroom and remove its
intermediate door.

## Spatial decision

D-066 combines the former bathing salon and service band as one continuous L-shaped
primary bathroom. The two model polygons remain only as a parametric tessellation of the
L shape; they no longer represent separate rooms.

| Control | Coordinated value |
| --- | ---: |
| Gross L-shaped area | **17.60 m²** |
| Schematic net area | **15.66 m²** |
| Fully open shared edge | **2.40 m** |
| Doors inside the bathroom | **0** |
| Suite access door | **1**, from the bedroom lounge |

The 15.66 m² net figure is an explicit schematic calculation: the 17.60 m² gross L shape
less a 0.10 m inward wet-wall allowance around its 19.80 m perimeter, with the inside
corner correction: `17.60 − (0.10 × 19.80) + 0.04 = 15.66 m²`. This is a coordination
value, not a construction measurement; selected wall build-ups and surveys must replace
it at detailed design.

## Layout and use

The full 2.40 m boundary between the two former zones is removed, including the jamb and
door leaf. One coordinated programme replaces the duplicated schematic fixtures:

- 1.45 × 1.55 m walk-in shower;
- 1.90 × 0.85 m freestanding tub zone;
- 2.50 m double vanity;
- WC with a short privacy screen; and
- 1.35 m linen cabinet.

The open connection improves daylight distribution and simultaneous use while retaining
a discreet WC position away from the principal bathing view. Exact fixture products,
service clearances, shower glass and accessibility remain subject to the 1:25 interior
design.

## Technical and cost gates

Detailed design must coordinate continuous falls and waterproofing, floor-drain and soil
routes, ventilation extraction and make-up air, hot-water demand, electrical safety,
lighting, acoustic plumbing treatment, maintenance access, privacy screening and the
structural/service condition where the partition is removed.

The deleted wall and door may reduce some partition and joinery scope, but that cannot be
booked as a saving before it is netted against waterproofing continuity, drainage,
screens, bathroom joinery and MEP changes. D-066 does not change the construction target
or authorize procurement or construction.
