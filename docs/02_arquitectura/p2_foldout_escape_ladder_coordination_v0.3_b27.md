# P2 rear rescue window and foldout escape ladder

**Status:** issued for D-082 schematic coordination; supplementary escape/rescue reserve;
not for construction  
**Version:** 0.3-b27 / R24  
**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Decision:** D-082  
**Source model:** `dreamhouse/p2_b27_delta.json`  
**Supersedes:** the inclined retractable-stair representation in P2 b26/R23 and earlier;
all D-080 wall geometry and D-081 corner graphics remain active  

## Controlled conclusion

The former inclined, counterbalanced exterior-stair sketch did not represent the device
requested by the owner. D-082 replaces it with an **operable rescue window beside a
wall-mounted vertical foldout escape ladder**. One rail remains fixed to the rear facade;
after a release accessible from the served opening, the second rail moves outward and
down under controlled gravity assist while the rungs rotate 90 degrees into use.

The ladder is stored vertically against the facade. It is not a telescoping inclined
flight, a free-falling assembly or a lower drop-down extension hung from another fire
escape. The mechanism, structure, release, corrosion protection and deployment control
must come from a tested proprietary system or an equivalently documented engineered
product; this record does not authorize bespoke fabrication.

## Plan-location check

The ladder is placed south of the new `W-EGRESS-P2` opening because the north side would
approach the existing `W-WELL` glazing at Y=13.00–17.00 m. Its 0.80 m along-wall clear
zone begins at Y=10.25 m. In the current PB b36 rear-facade geometry, the nearest lower
opening is `EXT-ESC`, ending at Y=9.65 m; the screened separation is therefore 0.60 m.

This is a cross-model clearance check only. Recheck after any change to PB doors, P2
windows, facade rails, drainage, exterior wiring, grade, utilities, landscape or rescue
access. The ladder may not pass in front of an opening below.

## Operation and security intent

- Release from the served P2 opening without a key, tool or dependence on electrical
  power.
- No operating handle accessible from grade.
- Controlled gravity-assisted deployment; no uncontrolled free fall.
- Fixed inner rail anchored to verified structure, not only to cladding or an unverified
  facade girt.
- Moving outer rail and rungs must deploy without striking glazing, flashings, doors,
  wiring, drainage or occupants below.
- Provide a safe transfer from the opening to the first rung, positive handholds and a
  fall-protection strategy. Add an engineered access platform if the selected opening and
  ladder cannot provide a safe direct transfer.

## Life-safety boundary

The system remains a **supplementary escape/rescue device**. It is not credited as the
required second exit, an accessible means of egress or a substitute for the permanent
stair. CF-012 remains open because NSR-10 requires a stair serving as a means of
evacuation to be fixed and permanent, while the proprietary foldout-ladder evaluation is
limited to use beside a code-complying emergency escape/rescue opening and excludes use
as the required means of egress.

The architect of record and the fire/life-safety authority must confirm occupancy,
required exits, rescue-opening geometry and hardware, signs, lighting, inspection,
maintenance, drills, emergency-service access and compatibility with the final facade.

## Research basis reviewed on 2026-08-21

- [ICC-ES ESR-4824 — Modum Foldable Fire Escape Ladders](https://cdn-v2.icc-es.org/wp-content/uploads/report-directory/ESR-4824.pdf): evaluated mechanism, use beside an operable emergency escape/rescue opening, load limits and conditions of use. This is product evidence, not Colombian approval.
- [JOMY retractable ladder specification guide](https://www.jomy.com/es/escaleras-verticales/retractil/jomy/guias-de-especificacion): closed profile, opening direction, rung geometry, support and clear-zone benchmarks.
- [JOMY access balcony](https://www.jomy.com/en-US/ladders/access-balcony): reference for an engineered transfer platform where direct window access is unsafe.
- [Colombia Decreto 340 de 2012 / NSR-10 amendments](https://www.suin-juriscol.gov.co/viewDocument.asp?id=1077639): permanent-stair and residential rescue-opening requirements that keep CF-012 open.

## Cost and procurement hold point

Use a proprietary tested assembly as the base pricing route. Obtain comparable delivered
and installed quotations that separate the operable window, ladder, mounting steel,
anchors, flashing, corrosion protection, transfer aids, freight, taxes and inspections.
Local fabrication may be compared only if it provides equivalent engineering,
documentation, testing, warranty and professional responsibility; low first cost alone is
not an acceptable life-safety basis.
