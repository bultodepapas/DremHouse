**Status:** active schematic coordination hypothesis under D-079; not for construction

**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** Project Car wall bench, RC/electronics wall bench, central RC assembly island,

**Sources:** owner instruction dated 2026-08-21; D-068, D-072, D-073 and D-078; PB

## Outcome

D-079 retains the two D-073 corner-start **9.00 × 0.75 m** wall-bench footprints and the
existing **4.50 × 1.60 m** central RC island. It stops treating the opposite wall benches
as equal work surfaces. Both use one economical six-module structural/service grammar,
but their task heights, surfaces, storage and services respond to different work.

The common coordination grid is **six replaceable 1.50 m modules per 9.00 m wall bench**.
This is a local-fabrication and phasing rule, not a selection of an imported proprietary
system. The central RC island uses three 1.50 m longitudinal modules.

Only R2–R4 form the ESD-controlled work area. Their dissipative surface, personnel
grounding and common point must be bonded to the electrical protective-earth system; no
independent intuitive ground stake is authorized. Solder extraction must capture at the
source. The existing LiPo activity remains separate from the ESD work surface.

## Window and envelope interface

Both technical windows retain a +0.90 m sill, matching the general top datum visually.
The alignment may not become a rigid physical connection. Test a maintainable **30–50 mm
shadow/service gap** or equivalent removable trough between worktop and window lining,
while preserving independent sill drainage, facade seals, cleaning and replacement.

The workbench frame remains dedicated bolted secondary steel. No worktop, vice, loaded
drawer, equipment or user load enters a glazing frame, door frame, unverified facade girt
or primary lateral system. The first 1.50 m module at each front corner remains a developed
door-jamb/track/guard/service transition until real door systems are selected.

## Lift and simultaneous-use conflict

The Project Car operating strip overlaps the current schematic lift/vehicle envelope by
approximately **1.10 m**. D-079 makes this overlap visible; it does not resolve it by
moving an unselected lift. The real lift, vehicle body and doors, arms, posts, adapters,
tool carts, drawer extension, emergency route and manufacturer bay layout must be tested
together before bench storage or services are frozen.

The current generic 0.10 m bench/envelope separation is graphic non-overlap only. The PB
programme's predecessor 0.12 m statement is superseded by the current b32/b36 geometry;
neither value is an operational clearance.

## RC support equipment and battery hold

The 3D-printer and LiPo schematic envelopes move 0.40 m inward, from Y=14.40–16.20 m to
Y=14.00–15.80 m. This leaves the 1.20 m RC wall-bench operating strip graphically clear.
It does not select printers, materials, extraction or a battery enclosure.

LiPo storage/charging requires a separate noncombustible, ventilated and monitored risk
strategy based on the real battery fleet, compatible chargers, quantities and local fire
review. It is not part of the ESD bench and may not obstruct the industrial door or escape.

## Research basis

External sources were checked on 2026-08-21 and remain benchmarks below Colombian law,
project decisions and professional design:

- [CCOHS standing-work ergonomics](https://www.ccohs.ca/oshanswers/ergonomics/standing/standing_basic.html): task-relative heights differ for precision, light and heavy downward-force work; adjustability and user fit govern.
- [Bott Cubio 2026 benchmark](https://www.bottltd.co.uk/content/download/93853/file/2026%20CNC_highlights%20brochure_web.pdf) and [LISTA workbench benchmark](https://app.lista.com/en/Workbench-27x36E-WxDxH-1500x750x890mm-4-drawers-1x502x1001x200-75kg-multiplex-KEY-Lock-Flame-red-RAL-3000-3250174.html): 0.75 m depth, approximately 0.84–0.90 m fixed heights and repeatable modules are credible heavy-duty references.
- [Treston WB electronics benchmark](https://www.treston.com/pdf/node/747): 0.80 m depth, +0.70–1.10 m adjustment and ESD-capable 1.50/1.80 m modules inform R2–R4.
- [ESD Association fundamentals](https://www.esda.org/assets/Documents/Fundamentals-of-ESD-Part-3-Basic-ESD-Control-Procedures-and-Materials.pdf): dissipative worksurface, personnel grounding and a common point are basic workstation elements.
- [HSE solder-fume guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/indg249.pdf): capture at source; a small fume-dispersing fan is not treated as adequate control.
- [Challenger SA10](https://challengerlifts.com/car_lift/10k-2-post-lift/) and [Rotary installation benchmark](https://rotarylift.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/manuals/two-post/SPOA7MP-Series/IN20613l%20%7C%20Install.pdf): lift dimensions and obstruction/bay requirements are product-specific.
- [CPSC battery guidance](https://www.cpsc.gov/Regulations-Laws--Standards/Voluntary-Standards/Topics/Batteries): high-energy batteries require compatible cells, packs, protection and chargers considered as a system.
- [Current Colombian RETIE source](https://www.minenergia.gov.co/es/misional/energia-electrica-2/reglamentos-tecnicos/reglamento-t%C3%A9cnico-de-instalaciones-el%C3%A9ctricas-retie/): final circuits, protection and grounding require legally competent electrical design.

## Cost and delivery control

D-079 changes no wall-bench length and no central-island footprint. It adds measurable
definition: twelve wall modules, three central-island modules, one lower heavy-force bay,
three manual-adjustable RC modules, localized ESD and solder-extraction scope, two 1.20 m
operating strips and a 0.40 m support-equipment shift.

The Project Car line `21.04`, central RC line `21.05` and technical storage line `21.06`
remain controls pending reconciliation. The 9.00 m RC wall bench is not a one-to-one priced
atomic item and must be recorded as an unpriced alert rather than silently absorbed.

Price economical local fabrication by module. Separate fixed rail/frame, replaceable top,
adjustable insert, cabinet, ESD surface, extraction and service distribution. Do not book a
saving or target increase before comparable quotations and a full-scale mock-up.
