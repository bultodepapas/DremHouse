## Outcome

D-078 replaces the separate Side A 3.00 m workstation window, 0.20 m junction and
4.30 m provisional main glazing with one centred architectural opening serving both the
shared workstation and the double-height hall. It also replaces the single-position
3.00 m Side A worktop with a two-position 5.40 m assembly and three suspended steel
drawer cabinets.

The concept is controlled from the interior. It organizes useful daylight, two permanent
work positions, storage, view and the scale of the monumental hall. It is not an exterior
composition exercise. Outside, the building must continue to read as one direct industrial
hall whose corrugated envelope, trims, glazing modules and drainage are selected for
resistance, weathering, replacement, maintenance and whole-life cost before appearance.

## Interior architectural reading

The former elevation read as two unrelated openings: a low 3.00 m workstation window,
an unresolved 0.20 m junction and a taller 4.30 m hall-glazing event. The worktop appeared
subordinate and displaced within the 10.50 m monumental band.

The new opening creates one legible internal horizon. The 0.90 m sill remains 0.15 m above
the worktop, leaving an insulated and maintainable upstand for seals, cable management and
the dedicated service rail. The three cabinet units establish a left/shared/right rhythm;
the centre cabinet divides the two work positions without adding a partition. Monitor arms,
two separated power/data zones and the final mullion positions must be coordinated so
primary sightlines are not blocked.

The 7.20 m opening meets the programme's 7–9 m main-glazing width identified by H-07 and
matches the nominal size of each technical-window family. This resolves the width portion
of H-07. It does not freeze relative visual hierarchy, glass performance, orientation or
solar control before the site is selected.

## Exterior and envelope principle

The exterior has deliberately low formal priority. Do not add a feature frame, projecting
portal, ornamental cladding change, domestic trim language or bespoke curtain-wall gesture
to celebrate this opening. The acceptable exterior reading is a rugged industrial wall
with one necessary modular opening.

Use the fewest durable, replaceable parts compatible with safety and performance:

- standard corrugated or profiled insulated-envelope modules;
- simple protected jamb, sill and head flashings;
- modular fixed glass with only the operable area required for ventilation strategy;
- robust coatings, seals and fasteners suitable for Boyacá exposure;
- downpipes and overflow paths retained in the opaque residual zones; and
- accessible components that can be replaced without dismantling the worktop or primary
  structure.

Side A remains the low eave and receives all roof water under D-039. The current downpipe
lines at X=10.50 and X=21.00 sit outside the 7.20 m opening, but their capacity, offsets,
guards and foundation drainage remain unverified. No visual preference may move water
across the opening or weaken the envelope.

## Structure, services and safety holds

The removed 0.20 m junction was only a trimmer reservation, not accepted structure.
Replacing it with one opening still requires a designed header, jambs, facade rails,
connections, deflection limits and a continuous longitudinal stability path. The opening
crosses the neutral X=12–18 m M60 test bay; the current structural model does not approve
that relationship.

The 5.40 m top, loaded cabinets and user equipment remain on a dedicated bolted
secondary-steel service frame. Do not load glass, window frames or unverified facade girts,
and do not field-weld the furniture frame to primary steel. Coordinate power/data
separation, accessible cable trays, task lighting, cabinet extension, locks, monitor arms,
cleaning, impact, corrosion, fire protection, vibration and replacement through a full-scale
mock-up.

Glare, blackout, privacy, condensation, cold downdraught, safe glass, operable areas,
cleaning and thermal bridges remain dependent on site orientation, real products and
professional envelope design.

## Cost measurement alert

The predecessor Side A openings measured approximately **18.495 m² gross**: 4.95 m² for
the 3.00 × 1.65 m workstation window plus 13.545 m² for the 4.30 × 3.15 m main glazing.
The unified 7.20 × 2.90 m test opening measures **20.88 m²**, an increase of approximately
**2.385 m² / 12.9%** before frame deductions.

At the same time, the perimeter of the two predecessor openings falls from approximately
24.20 m to 20.20 m for the single test opening, a reduction of approximately **4.00 m /
16.5%** before internal mullions. This may reduce separate head/sill/jamb, seal and flashing
work, but no saving is recognized because the longer header, safe-glass specification and
module design remain unquoted.

The Side A worktop grows from 2.70 m² to 4.86 m², an increase of 2.16 m². One cabinet with
three drawers is added, taking the complete PB workstation family from four to five
cabinet units and from twelve to fifteen drawers. Chapters 12, 13 and 21 require
remeasurement and comparable quotations. D-078 does not change the cost target.
