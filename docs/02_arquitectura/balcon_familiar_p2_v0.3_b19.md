## Owner decision and selected alternative

The owner requested removal of the family-facing wall so the overlook reads and feels as
one substantially larger balcony. P2 b19/R16 therefore removes the complete X=21 wall and
acoustic glazing from **Y=5.00 to Y=12.45**, creating a **7.45 m open frontage** toward the
double-height hall.

Three alternatives were evaluated:

1. retain the R15 acoustic enclosure and enlarge only the glazed opening—best acoustic
   performance, but it does not deliver the requested open-balcony experience;
2. open only the 3.60 m Phase 1 family-room edge—lower intervention, but leaves the old
   mini-deck and Phase 2 study edge visually fragmented; and
3. **selected:** join the former mini deck, family distributor and Phase 2 study edge
   behind one 7.45 m open guarded frontage.

Opening all 18.00 m was rejected because it would expose both children's bedrooms directly
to the hall and contradict their privacy, acoustic and fire-control requirements.

## Spatial result

The former mini deck, family distributor and study edge retain their traceable model IDs
but work as one connected **59.745 m² gross L-shaped family balcony/lounge/distributor**.
The former 1.00 m door between DECK and FAM becomes a full **2.80 m opening**, and
GLZ-DECK is removed. No residual wall pier is retained along the family frontage.

P2-W04R remains full height only at the bedroom ends:

- Y=0.00–5.00 at Child Suite 1; and
- Y=12.45–18.00 at Child Suite 2.

Both suites remain enclosed and require acoustically controlled entrance doors.

## Guard, structure and phasing

A continuous **1.10 m minimum schematic guard** is reserved along the full open edge. Its
height, loads, openings, non-climbability, impact resistance, anchors and interface with
the edge beam require professional design. The exposed X=21 truss, floor vibration, fire
protection and falling-object control must be coordinated with that guard.

During Phase 1, the open edge runs from Y=5.00 to Y=11.00. When Phase 2 is completed, it
extends to Y=12.45. Temporary construction and occupancy conditions must provide compliant
guarding at every stage.

## Acoustic and fire consequence

D-063 intentionally supersedes D-058 continuity across the family frontage. The family
centre is no longer acoustically separated from the hall/workshops, and hall sound will
be perceptible there. Smoke movement, workshop separation and the effect on suite entrance
doors require professional fire and acoustic review. No performance rating is claimed.

## Cost and authority

The change removes approximately 7.45 m of full-height acoustic wall and the GLZ-DECK
system, but adds a long engineered guard, edge/anchor coordination and potentially stronger
suite doors, smoke-control measures or local rated returns. No saving is recognized until
these scopes are measured and quoted. The drawing is not suitable for procurement,
fabrication or construction.
