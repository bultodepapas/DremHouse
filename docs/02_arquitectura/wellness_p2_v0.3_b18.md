## Spatial decision

P2 b18/R15 eliminates the remaining 4.50 × 1.45 m rear spur as a separately named
corridor and absorbs its **6.525 m² gross** into the wellness programme. Together with
the existing 2.90 × 5.55 m sauna/shower room, it forms one L-shaped **22.62 m² gross
dry/wet wellness suite**.

The former spur becomes the dry threshold for changing, towel/equipment storage and
transition into the wet/heat zone. A 2.90 m opening joins it visually and functionally to
the sauna/shower room. The main room adds a cooling/recline bench, so the recovered area
has a programme and no longer reads as residual circulation.

## Access and egress control

A continuous **1.20 m schematic clear lane** remains through the dry threshold to the
rear retractable exterior-stair reserve. This is a geometric coordination reserve only.
A responsible fire professional must confirm whether an exit route may cross this dry
wellness threshold or whether a physically separated rated clear lane is required.

Wet and dry functions remain distinct: the threshold is not a shower or sauna surface,
and the existing room retains the shower, sauna and cooling/recline furniture. Final
floor falls, drainage, waterproofing, slip resistance, ventilation, heat isolation and
door/air-transfer strategy require developed design.

## Controls retained

- unchanged 18.00 × 15.00 m / 270 m² P2 envelope;
- D-061 family distributor and open Phase 2 study edge;
- exactly four ensuite bedrooms and unchanged D-042 child-bedroom geometry;
- protected-stair footprint and four D-048 column reservations;

- P2-W01, P2-W04 and P2-W05 controls under D-057–D-059; and
- all structural, acoustic, envelope, site-orientation and second-exit gates.

## Cost and authority

R15 removes a corridor finish category but expands the wellness finish and equipment
scope by 6.525 m². Waterproofing limits, resilient/slip-resistant flooring, ventilation,
joinery, lighting, towel storage and any rated route separation must be measured and
quoted. D-062 changes neither the cost target nor contingency. The sheets are
coordination evidence only and are not suitable for procurement or construction.
