**Status:** owner-directed refinement; option only; not adopted and not for construction  
**Version:** 0.3-option-32-PB  
**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** true centring of the schematic Project Car, lift posts and exclusion envelope within the usable Side A technical bay  
**Source:** owner instruction of 2026-08-21; PB b31 option; active PB b29 and D-073

## Architectural conclusion

Centre the **complete Project Car/lift group**, rather than moving the vehicle symbol by
itself. The proposed vehicle, symmetric post pair and 6.40 × 5.87 m lift/exclusion
envelope now share the coordination centre **X=5.34 m, Y=3.965 m**.

The governing horizontal territory is not the raw 10.50 × 7.00 m Side A bay. Its usable
area begins behind the 0.75 m-deep wall bench, at Y=0.93 m, and ends before the Y=7.00 m
central pedestrian axis. Its exact geometric centre is **X=5.34 m, Y=3.965 m**. The lift
envelope is centred on that point in both directions, leaving equal graphic residuals to
the bench and central axis. The schematic envelope is 0.03 m shallower than its
predecessor so both residuals meet the inherited 0.10 m graphic-separation check.

## Architectural and technical notes

- The centring reference excludes the fixed Side A wall bench. Centring within the raw
  bay would ignore occupied floor depth and would not describe the actual usable space.
- The car, posts and exclusion envelope share one centre. This avoids the predecessor
  condition in which the car symbol was offset inside an already off-centre envelope.
- The Y=7.00–11.00 m central pedestrian axis remains unobstructed. No storage, tool cart
  or open vehicle door may project into it.
- The equal 0.10 m bench and axis residuals are only graphic non-overlap controls. They are
  not accepted working or life-safety clearances.
- Replace all schematic rectangles with the selected vehicle and lift data before
  developed design. Verify doors, lift arms, controls, hood and trunk access, tool carts,
  egress, slab loads, anchors, drainage, power and compressed-air routing.

## Cost and governance

b32 changes only the placement of schematic symbols and does not change the lift type,
building area, bench length or active works. It therefore does not modify the cost
baseline. Real lift selection, reinforced slab requirements, anchors, drainage or service
changes must be measured if the option is adopted.

PB b32 supersedes b31 only for the Project Car/lift position. PB b29 remains the active
published ground-floor plan, and D-073 remains the adopted workbench basis. This option
does not create D-074, modify the current drawing catalog or freeze a lift product or
manufacturer clearance.
